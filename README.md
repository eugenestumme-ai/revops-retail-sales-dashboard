# Retail Sales Performance & Operations Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built in Google Looker Studio designed to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), track time-series revenue trends, and analyze category-level sales performance for retail operations.

## 🔑 Key Features & Business Metrics
* **Executive Scorecards:** Real-time tracking of total revenue, profit margins, order volume, and average order value (AOV).
* **Time-Series Revenue Trends:** Visualizing monthly and daily sales trajectories to spot seasonal spikes, sales velocity, and operational dips.
* **Category & Regional Performance:** Breakdown bar charts tracking high-performing product categories and geographic sales distribution.
* **Interactive Cross-Filtering:** Enabled multi-chart cross-filtering so stakeholders can click any data point to instantly isolate specific regions, product lines, or date ranges.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Data Architecture
* **BI Tool:** Google Looker Studio
* **Data Source:** Structured CSV / Google Sheets backend modeling retail transactions.
* **Methodology:** Dimensional data modeling, KPI metric definitions, calculated fields, and responsive UX design tailored for operational decision-making.
