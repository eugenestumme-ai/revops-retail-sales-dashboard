# Retail Sales Performance & Operations Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built in Google Looker Studio designed to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), track time-series revenue trends, and analyze category-level sales performance for retail operations.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview & Overview
* **Key Visuals Included:** Geographic sales distribution by state, top 5 product categories (led by Furniture and Electronics), monthly sales trends scaling past $70K in revenue, and a unit price vs. units sold scatter plot segmented by customer type.
* **Full Report Document:** You can view the complete exported report layout [directly here in the repository](./Retail_Sales_Performance_Dashboard.pdf.pdf).

## 🚀 Live Demo & Access
* [View Live Looker Studio Dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/e27b39ab-c32b-472e-8b32-65e9ccbb928b)

## 🔑 Key Features & Business Metrics
* **Executive Scorecards:** Real-time tracking of total revenue ($71,083), total units sold (468), and performance by state.
* **Time-Series Revenue Trends:** Visualizing monthly sales trajectories to track growth velocity.
* **Category & Regional Performance:** Breakdown charts tracking high-performing product lines and geographic distribution.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Data Architecture
* **BI Tool:** Google Looker Studio
* **Methodology:** Dimensional data modeling, KPI metric definitions, calculated fields, and responsive UX design tailored for operational decision-making.
